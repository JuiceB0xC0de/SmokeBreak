# Games

Actual games you play in the terminal. Only the strange or excellent ones.

27 entries. [Back to the shelf](README.md)

## quick

- **[Frotz](../drafts/brew/frotz.md)**: A small C interpreter that runs Infocom's classic text adventures and other Z-machine story files in your terminal.  
  `ancient, game, infocom, interactive-fiction, terminal, z-machine`
- **[robotfindskitten](../drafts/brew/robotfindskitten.md)**: A tiny zen terminal game where a robot walks a field of symbols looking for a kitten, ported to dozens of platforms since 1997.  
  `ancient, game, tui, zen`
- **[ski](../drafts/brew/ski.md)**: A silly terminal skiing game where your jet-powered skis are on backwards, so you flee a Yeti while only seeing the slope behind you.  
  `ancient, game, python, tui, unix-history`
- **[Wumpus](../drafts/brew/wumpus.md)**: An exact clone of Gregory Yob's 1973 Hunt the Wumpus, bad interface included, plus a bundled satire called superhack.  
  `basic, game, history, retrocomputing`
- **[zBoy](../drafts/brew/zboy.md)**: A small open-source Game Boy emulator that can link two virtual consoles over a LAN and rebuild hi-score tables for games with no battery save.  
  `emulator, gameboy, retro, sdl, two-player`

## afternoon

- **[Allure of the Stars](../drafts/brew/allureofthestars.md)**: A near-future sci-fi roguelike where you command a whole squad, with deterministic combat and bullets you can sidestep, written in Haskell.  
  `game, haskell, roguelike, squad, tactics`
- **[Chocolate Doom](../drafts/brew/chocolate-doom.md)**: A source port of Doom that tries to behave exactly like the 1993 DOS original, bugs included, and also runs Heretic, Hexen and Strife.  
  `ancient, doom, game, preservation, source-port`
- **[CorsixTH](../drafts/brew/corsixth.md)**: An open source rebuild of Theme Hospital, the 1997 hospital management sim, that runs on modern systems with zoom, 4K and a map editor.  
  `game, gui, lua, reimplementation, simulation`
- **[dungeon](../drafts/brew/dungeon.md)**: The original MIT text adventure that became Zork, in Robert Supnik's 1980 FORTRAN port, updated so it builds with gfortran today.  
  `fortran, game, history, text-adventure, zork`
- **[EasyRPG Player](../drafts/brew/easyrpg-player.md)**: An open-source interpreter that runs RPG Maker 2000 and 2003 games on modern systems, and in a browser, without the original engine.  
  `emulation, game, gui, interpreter, retro, rpg-maker`
- **[Exult](../drafts/brew/exult.md)**: An open-source engine that runs Ultima VII, the early-1990s RPG, on modern systems by reading your own copy of the original game files.  
  `ancient, game, game-engine, gui, preservation, rpg`
- **[Fairy-Stockfish](../drafts/brew/fairy-stockfish.md)**: A Stockfish-derived engine that plays Xiangqi, Shogi, Janggi, Crazyhouse and any chess-like game you define in a config file.  
  `chess, engine, game, uci, variants`
- **[fairymax](../drafts/brew/fairymax.md)**: A small chess engine built to play fairy chess variants, with separate executables for chess-like games such as Xiangqi and Shogi.  
  `chess, engine, game-ai, variants`
- **[fheroes2](../drafts/brew/fheroes2.md)**: An open-source engine that runs Heroes of Might and Magic II from its original data files, with higher resolution and a better AI.  
  `game, gui, remake, reverse-engineering, sdl2, strategy`
- **[Homeworlds](../drafts/brew/homeworlds.md)**: A C++ framework, command-line player and GUI for Binary Homeworlds, a two-player "space chess" played with pyramids and no board.  
  `ai, c++, game, library, strategy`
- **[Liberal Crime Squad](../drafts/brew/lcs.md)**: A satirical terminal strategy RPG where you recruit Elite Liberal radicals to stop the United States sliding into Arch-Conservatism.  
  `game, oubliette, rpg, satire, strategy, terminal`
- **[netris](../drafts/brew/netris.md)**: A two-player terminal Tetris played over a network, where clearing lines sends garbage rows to your opponent, with a documented protocol for writing bots.  
  `game, multiplayer, ncurses, network, tui`
- **[open-adventure](../drafts/brew/open-adventure.md)**: A faithful, authorized port of Colossal Cave Adventure, the 1970s cave-exploring text game that started a genre, playable as `advent` in your terminal.  
  `game, history, interactive-fiction, text-adventure`
- **[Pachi](../drafts/brew/pachi.md)**: An open-source Go engine built on Monte Carlo tree search, showing how game AI played Go before AlphaGo, and it still plays strongly.  
  `ai, game, go, gtp, mcts`
- **[Pioneer](../drafts/brew/pioneer.md)**: A free, open-source space sandbox where you fly between star systems, land on planets, and make a living by trading, smuggling, piracy, or bounty hunting.  
  `game, gui, open-source, simulation, space`
- **[Simon Tatham's Portable Puzzle Collection](../drafts/brew/puzzles.md)**: About forty small one-player logic puzzles, each its own command, from Simon Tatham's collection. Type a name and a puzzle window opens.  
  `game, gui, logic, puzzle`
- **[rpg-cli](../drafts/brew/rpg-cli.md)**: A tiny JRPG where cd is the movement command: the deeper you wander from home, the tougher the monsters, and your filesystem becomes the dungeon.  
  `game, rpg, rust, shell, tui`
- **[ScummVM](../drafts/brew/scummvm.md)**: ScummVM replaces the executables of hundreds of classic adventure games so LucasArts, Sierra and other old titles run natively on modern systems.  
  `adventure, game, gui, preservation, retro`
- **[Simutrans](../drafts/brew/simutrans.md)**: A long-running open-source transport simulator where you build rail, road, ship and air networks, and swappable paksets turn one engine into many different games.  
  `game, gui, multiplayer, open-source, simulation, transport`
- **[Supermodel](../drafts/brew/supermodel.md)**: An emulator for Sega's Model 3 arcade boards, rebuilt entirely from reverse engineering because Sega never published documentation for the hardware.  
  `arcade, emulator, games, reverse-engineering, sega`
- **[zork](../drafts/brew/zork.md)**: The public-domain mainframe Zork, called Dungeon, translated from FORTRAN to C so you can play the older, larger game on a modern terminal.  
  `game, history, public-domain, terminal, text-adventure`
